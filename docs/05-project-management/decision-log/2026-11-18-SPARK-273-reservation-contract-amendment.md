The existing effective interval, buffers, protected-claim ownership, H/T, F and allocation rules, cancellation rights, physical-session rules and reconciliation/compensation retain their approved semantics for the applicable lifecycle. No new worker, scheduler, broker plugin, retry state machine or receipt-coordination table is authorized. Wire compatibility and owner review remain implementation gates. For the future covered branch, H is the reservation hold deadline `min(hold creation + effective reservation hold duration, T)`. Require authoritative acceptance and valid protection at the guarded evaluation strictly before H. If expiry wins, release the hold and require renewed review and a new request. Later acceptance may be reconciled without restoring entitlement. The compatible pre-T reacquisition exception is for verified late paid money. ## Settlement amendment and open decisions · 20 November 2026

- **Approved reviewed settlement.** Payment records the full obligation as financially paid when one exact, verified and correctly bound transfer satisfies a pending positive-value order, including after H. Only that transition publishes `PaymentSucceededV1`. Operations retains the approved H/T, Facility and capacity guards. [Payment authority handoff](../handoffs/2026-11-19-SPARK-273-payment-authority.md#settlement-and-compatibility)
- **Open cancelled-order lifecycle.** Keep a genuinely cancelled order cancelled, preserve the authentic collection, and record a disposition using its transaction ID. Future approval is required for reversing cancellation, independently modeling financial settlement and commercial cancellation, cancellation races, customer status/explanations, and final refund or compensation policy.
- **Open notification wording.** NotificationAudit currently sends an exit grace-period instruction for the shared payment event, including reviewed reservation settlements. An approved follow-up should give reservation customers wording appropriate to their booking and avoid implying confirmation or exit eligibility. The existing parking-session notification and shared event wire shape remain available.
