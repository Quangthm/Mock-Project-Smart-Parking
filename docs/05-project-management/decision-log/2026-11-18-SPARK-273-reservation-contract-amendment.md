# SPARK-273 — Reservation architecture and contract amendment

**Decision date:** 18 November 2026 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)  
**Status:** Approved design baseline from the user’s SPARK-273 Slice 1 instructions. Source inspection at `3b60787d06f3452fff276f622219de1659ee49d0`. Runtime verification pending.  
**Jira:** [SPARK-273 / US-D01](https://mangamanagement.atlassian.net/browse/SPARK-273). Its earlier overlap and zero-price criteria are refined by D18-D and D18-F. Normal allocation remains part of the broader story and outside this documentation slice.

**Scope:** CAR / Specific Slot, quote review through durable confirmation. Subscription-covered confirmation is an approved future contract.

| Decision | Rationale and ownership | Supersession |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D18-A. Use canonical plate, authoritative slot-derived site/tenant and original Account. UserService checks active, unambiguous plate ownership during admission. Later transfer/removal alone leaves accepted reservations valid. | Operations owns booking/claim and customer access. UserService owns registered vehicles. Trusted gate evidence identifies the physical vehicle. | Replaces client vehicle/site IDs and VehicleId-based binding in the 9 November creation workflow and prior API. Retains canonical plate, the accepted binding read-to-commit race and the separate walk-in policy. |
| D18-B. Require explicit acceptance of a Payment-owned immutable quote before admission. Operations resolves slot context over authenticated Facility HTTP and evaluates prospective plate coverage. | Customers review authoritative terms before capacity is held. Operations owns entitlement and rechecks coverage under its final guard. The initial eligibility implementation returns false. | Replaces Operations terms as the commercial authority and any Account-based coverage assumption. Retains accepted operational policy context. |
| D18-C. Use an EF-backed MassTransit Saga StateMachine with concurrent Account-current, plate and Facility checks. A definitive rejection closes admission logically even with replies in flight. | Operations owns the durable orchestration, correlation and terminal fence. UserService and FacilityService own their respective answers. | Replaces bespoke polling orchestration and sequential vehicle/Facility dependency checks as the admission coordinator. Extends D11-A’s short receipt to both committed hold paths. |
| D18-D. Reject overlapping active claims for the same normalized plate at the same site, across slots. Permit overlapping future bookings across sites, subject to physical-session and operational guards. | Operations preserves compatible capacity and physical exclusivity as independent constraints. | Supersedes global future interval exclusivity in FR-RES-02, BR-RES-02 and the 9 November reservation overlap guidance. |
| D18-E. Use authenticated SignalR with committed, versioned status and a recovery read on initial connection, reconnect or explicit restoration. | Operations owns the authoritative projection. Event-driven updates report check and financial readiness without repeated status requests. | Supersedes polling in the earlier API, proposal, handoffs and any reservation Payment return loop. |
| D18-F. Commit hold and owned outbox trigger atomically, finalize admission, then provision the positive deposit or obtain future covered acceptance. | Durable local scheduling survives process loss. Payment owns the paid order, provider and collection, or a genuine zero-value order and durable receipt/invoice evidence. Operations alone confirms under guards. | Preserves D11-B verified paid collection and replaces its covered no-order path. Covered acceptance returns over authenticated idempotent HTTP to the Operations consumer, with no Payment acceptance event. |
| D18-G. Preserve Facility protection and confirmation verification until the competing-writer proof passes. | Eligibility can become stale. Operations and Facility jointly own the protection boundary. | Retains D11-C’s conditional gate and the 10 November database evaluation cutoff. Confirmation updates reservation/projection and its outbox, while admission stays terminal. |

The existing effective interval, buffers, protected-claim ownership, H/T, F and allocation rules, cancellation rights, physical-session rules and reconciliation/compensation retain their approved semantics. No new worker, scheduler, broker plugin, retry state machine or receipt-coordination table is authorized. Wire compatibility and owner review remain implementation gates.
