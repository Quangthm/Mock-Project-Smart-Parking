## Booking and confirmation

| Checkpoint | Owner and durable effect |
| --- | --- |
| Review and consent | The driver reviews a Payment-issued price for a canonical plate and a Facility-resolved slot. The existing quote request captures the initiating User and Account, quote ID/version, normalized selection, idempotency key and consent time. The trusted proof reader must return the original immutable issuance and current consumption status. A current-price calculation cannot satisfy this port. |
| Admission join | The three correlated successes enqueue evaluation through the existing Saga consumer outbox. The new internal Operations queue `operations-reservation-evaluate-v1` dispatches a MediatR command outside the Saga's pessimistic transaction. A terminal wake targets the existing Saga queue. The evaluator also accepts redelivery of an older evaluation already addressed to the Saga. The existing authorization worker can recover a due evaluation. |
| Facility protection | The evaluator prepares the existing durable ADMISSION token. The dependency worker obtains the owner snapshot outside its local transaction. Recording HELD also enqueues evaluation for the quote receipt. Complete and abort still use the same token, immutable intent and local outcome. A terminal receipt does not discharge the token worker's obligation. |
| Quote hold | The handler fetches Payment proof before opening its transaction. It locks the receipt and token, then Account, canonical plate and site. It compares scope, interval, slot, method, quote ID/version, issued/unconsumed status, validity, tariff revision, FULL whole-VND terms, local coverage, Facility version and effective policies. Restrictions, overlap, open physical visits and capacity are evaluated under the guards. A final database checkpoint tests deadlines and policy effectiveness. The protected row, H, HOLD_CREATED receipt, READY token and hold/availability outbox effects share one commit. The original Account guard serializes contenders. A unique quote ID also protects the stored binding across versions. |
| Provisioning | The hold consumer dispatches through MediatR. The handler reloads the reservation and uses its accepted quote fields or historical terms as appropriate. Exact operation, reservation, order binding, amount, currency, method and H travel to the owner. Uncertain outcomes use the original identity and the existing due queue. A first attempt remains fenced by quote expiry and H pending an owner decision. |
| Paid evidence | Only the internal authoritative deposit command can decide a quote booking. The generic payment consumer still handles legacy rows. It cannot fill missing financial identity or quote evidence for a new row. Exact replay checks collection identity, binding and the fingerprint of the transaction and verified provider reference/time. A different collection or conflicting evidence goes to the owner's idempotent reconciliation path, including after confirmation. A busy delivery must retry. |
| Confirmation | Paid and zero commands share one Application handler, workflow lease, database transaction and CONFIRMATION token protocol. The normal Domain transition requires the held slot and a checkpoint strictly before H. A separate Domain transition allows a released paid claim to compete strictly before T after fresh guarded eligibility, policy, plate and capacity checks. An unavailable claim grants no entitlement. A later policy revision may still permit reacquisition. The original interval and protected buffer must satisfy the current policy, and the availability check uses its current occupancy projection and allocation lead time. |
| Covered or genuine zero | Tests supply a trusted owner for the exact zero binding, a durable zero order ID and receipt/issuance ID. Missing or contradictory evidence stays unknown. A definitive rejection releases the hold. The accepted order and receipt are persisted together before guarded confirmation. Expiry or cancellation wins against late evidence. Recovery reconciles the same commercial record and never reacquires capacity for it. A new booking requires a fresh review and request. |
| Recovery | Expiry, order provisioning, zero acceptance, confirmation and token completion/abort reuse the current workers. A first collection or accepted zero order denied entitlement retains its reconciliation reason and due state until the owner acknowledges it. Distinct/conflicting collections depend on redelivery and idempotent Payment acceptance if the immediate reconciliation call fails. A reconciliation acknowledgement records disposition, and does not establish payout. |

Facility slot lookup, admission observation and token acquisition all use the existing resource snapshot hash. One competing writer needed correction. A realtime occupancy projection could set a slot to maintenance or unavailable while a token held its previous operational state. Projection now shares the site fence before its watermark/slot write. Its MediatR handler owns the Facility UoW. A busy token rolls back the event receipt, and the existing worker can redeliver the same event. Facility's HTTP contracts, error mapping, signed slot lookup, physical Gate implementation and schema needed no changes.

## Compatibility and rollout

The grouped confirmation source was split before changing behavior. The paid and zero confirmation handlers had duplicated locking, deadline, dependency and publication logic. Both command types now dispatch to `ConfirmReservationHandler`. The generic payment translator, authoritative deposit handler, deposit evidence, dependency-advance handler, and availability command/handler have dedicated files. The hold consumer's former static routing helper moved into `ProcessReservationHoldHandler`. The quote review now uses `IMediator`. No replacement store or commercial terms table was introduced.

The VehicleId/ReservationTerms ingress, serializers, idempotency fingerprints, legacy preparation worker and terms repository still serve existing clients and in-flight requests. Legacy bookings retain their terms FK, pricing/policy snapshots, request and vehicle references. Own list/detail and by-order recovery use the existing repository and Account scope. Provisioning and generic payment confirmation still read historical terms for old orders. The separate assistant own-read stack and discovery catalogue entries R1–R7 have the same evidence as the develop review.

Fresh Operations initialization creates two booking shapes in the original `03b` script. Quote rows carry the immutable quote reference, validity, tariff and coverage decision, accepted amounts and operational policy. The receipt preserves actor/consent and Facility decision evidence. Zero order/receipt and collection fingerprint fields support settlement replay. Legacy rows keep their existing financial history. The booking completeness check, unique quote/order/collection IDs, plate exclusion and physical-session constraints apply alongside the application guards. Direct SQL, bulk writes and direct DbContext saves bypass UoW-only immutability and outbox guarantees.

**Existing databases require a separately approved transition before this binary is deployed**, even with new ingress gated. EF reads the additional columns on historical rows. Re-running CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS does not add columns or change existing checks/indexes. Database and service owners must inspect actual schema and pending workflows, approve an additive transition and rehearse historical reads, overlapping releases, upgrades and rollback. Never infer a quote, plate, coverage decision or provider identity for a historical row. Retirement requires a client cutover and drain or approved archival of old reviews, admitted requests, pending holds/orders, disputed collections, outbox work and history. No existing database was upgraded.

## Production activation conditions

- **Payment quote owner.** Agree the authenticated Account-scoped proof route, exact persisted response, consumption state and safe errors for missing, stale and unavailable proof. Prove one atomic quote consumption with order creation and exact operation replay. Resolve whether a timely local acceptance remains provisionable after quote expiry but before H.
- **Payment collections owner.** Deliver authenticated versioned evidence for every distinct verified collection, including additional money after terminal status. Supply an equivalent targeted lookup for lost acknowledgements and recovery, plus durable idempotent reconciliation. Today's generic event and placeholder lookup cannot attest the original quote and collection. Validate order, Account/scope, method, amount/currency, transaction, stable collection identity, provider reference/time and quote ID/version in the paired service tests.
- **Payment covered acceptance owner.** Agree the zero order/receipt operation, durable receipt or issuance obligation, exact binding replay, loss recovery, the H fence and commercial reconciliation after expiry. The in-process types describe facts required by Operations. They are not an agreed Payment wire protocol.
- **Operations and deployment owners.** Verify signed owner exchanges, restricted sender/receiver permissions including the internal evaluation queue, outbox relay, missed-delivery redrive and hard-crash recovery with the actual owners. Release and verify each schema transition first. Production DI continues to supply the unavailable admission starter and unavailable quote owner.
