## Operations and integration transport

Use service-local versioned MassTransit types and compatible wire identities. The durable start carries workflow/request identity. Read-only requests logically carry the workflow, stable branch operation, attempt correlation, deadline and the minimum trusted input below. Responses echo attempt and binding and carry owner decision, evaluated time/version where applicable, and a safe reason. Validate allowed producer, message identity, active attempt and matching immutable input. Retransmission uses the same logical operation identity; stale or mismatched envelopes never update a terminal request. Use existing Account-current topology and decision replay. New plate and slot wire names, topology and ACLs need joint owner review. Publish durable branch intent without synchronous broker RPC or an open browser request.

| Interaction | Owner and required content |
| --- | --- |
| Account-current request/decision | UserService evaluates current Account/role for the trusted actor and reservation policy. Preserve one durable decision per workflow, delivery-attempt fences and authorization ADR budgets. |
| Plate validation/result | UserService consumes initiating Account and candidate plate; returns authoritative canonical plate and active eligible CAR decision. |
| Slot validation/result | FacilityService consumes selected slot GUID and required eligibility context; returns authoritative site/tenant, slot, eligibility and configuration evidence. |
| Hold committed | Operations outbox publishes an existing-style hold fact/trigger with event identity/version, request/reservation/claim reference, scope and H; consumer reloads persisted binding. Preserve existing v1 compatibility or agree a versioned transition. |
| Verified paid collection | Require an agreed Payment-owned versioned reservation collection contract with explicit collection identity and immutable quote binding. Current `PaymentSucceededV1` wiring is a source baseline. The Payment owner must demonstrate equivalent complete evidence and approve the contract, or agree a versioned event. Keep parking-session routing separate. |
| Covered acceptance | Operations owns durable trigger and calls Payment’s authenticated HTTP endpoint. Authoritative idempotent HTTP response is the only success evidence from Payment for this handoff. |

Current wiring in `ReservationMessaging` is configuration-gated. Reservation admission enables the Account decision, hold-changed and reservation payment consumers and the three due-work workers. The paid event exchange is `smartpark.payment.payment-succeeded.v1` and the reservation queue is `smartpark-operation-reservation-payment-succeeded-v1`. Hold delivery uses `smartpark.operations.reservation-hold-changed.v1` to `smartpark-operation-reservation-hold-changed-v1`. The restricted-broker profile registers the EF Bus Outbox on `ReservationDbContext`; relay configuration can disable delivery. Account decisions have EF consumer outbox middleware. Hold and reservation payment consumers have interval retries of 1, 5 and 15 seconds in source. No reservation EF Saga StateMachine or reservation progress hub registration was found. Verify topology, enablement and post-commit delivery under the chosen deployment profile.

The current `ReservationRequestsController` returns HTTP 202 with `Location` and receipt version 2. `CreateReservationRequest` expects VehicleId, SiteId, RequestedSlotId, mode, interval, TermsId/version and method. Signed-in Driver DTOs and existing persisted requests require a compatibility plan before adopting the target shape.

## UserService — admission slice

Reuse registration, normalization, eligibility and authoritative repository rules. Add an Account-and-plate lookup contract for the asynchronous validation branch. Missing, inactive, transferred away, ambiguous or other-Account registrations and unsupported vehicle class are definitive ineligible outcomes without exposing another Account. Transport/data-access failure is a dependency outcome. Normalize using the same canonicalization used by registration and Operations; compare owner-issued canonical plate to quote binding. A lookup is an observation, with freshness/transfer-race verification required. Preserve original Account access and trusted plate-based gate identity after booking. Verify a transfer visible before the ownership check causes denial and a post-booking transfer leaves customer access and booking intact.

Source today: `VehicleBindingsController` and `ResolveVehicleBindingQuery` resolve by Account plus VehicleId and optional expected revision; the handler returns INELIGIBLE or ACTIVE with canonical plate. `ProfileMessagingRegistration` wires the Account-current consumer conditionally. A registered plate-validation consumer and owner-issued plate lookup for this Saga are target work.

## FacilityService — quote and admission slices

Provide the [slot-only HTTP and correlated MassTransit validation contracts](../../03-design/api/reservation-facility-dependency-api-design.md). Resolve through the owning slot/resource repository. Include authoritative tenant and site, scoped slot facts, category/status/eligibility and compatible configuration evidence. Return definitive ineligibility for absent/disabled/incompatible resources, distinct from an unavailable dependency. No physical gate claim during review or validation.

Today `ReservationDependenciesController` exposes site+slot facts and acquire/complete/abort. `ReservationDependencyRepository` owns facts/protection and `OperatorGateSlotRepository` invokes the existing dependency guard. The slot-only HTTP and Facility admission consumer are target interfaces. Keep token fencing and its durable resolution. Verify every supported Facility/Operations/Gate writer, delayed invalidation, retries, restarts and same-resource competition before considering a live-claim fast path. A slot result or a HELD status alone cannot establish that invariant.

## PaymentService — quote and confirmation slices

Use the [PaymentService confirmation handoff](2026-11-18-SPARK-273-payment-confirmation-handoff.md) for separate paid and covered evidence contracts, source findings and release gates.

**Quote.** Keep Payment’s quote entity and owner pricing calculation. Version and bind canonical plate, authoritative tenant/site/slot, interval, method, initiating Account, coverage/version, tariff, amounts/currency, validity and applicable terms. Preserve agreed rounding/prepayment rules. Accept a prospective Operations entitlement assertion tied to plate/site/interval; price the covered and payable amounts and disclose them distinctly. Coverage follows the canonical plate, site and interval across UserService registration changes. The initiating Account remains part of consent, purchase access and audit. Initial coverage is false; inability to get an authoritative quote is unavailable. Operations uses persisted trusted quote evidence during the guarded join, without calling Payment inside the transaction. Agree proof retrieval and allowed validity/consumption semantics before implementation.

**Paid path.** Reuse `PUT /internal/reservation-deposit-orders/{operationId}` and existing trusted machine authentication. Atomically accept the exact reviewed quote and create/replay a single reservation order under stable operation identity, exact H, and immutable booking/binding/amount/currency/method. Replays return persisted owner evidence, changed requests conflict, and new order/checkout creation at or after H is fenced. An earlier order that is now closed may still have a collection to reconcile. Preserve real payOS checkout and provider verification. Finalize the paid event and recovery lookup with the [PaymentService owner handoff](2026-11-18-SPARK-273-payment-confirmation-handoff.md#paid-deposits--open-event-contract-gate) before relying on them for target confirmation.

**Future fully covered path.** Agree an authenticated idempotent HTTP operation with Payment ownership of route and wire fields. Bind operation/reservation IDs, original Account, plate, tenant/site/slot, interval/method, quote/version, coverage/version, currency, zero due and applicable deadline. Persist or replay one genuine linked zero-value order/reference, acceptance evidence and a durable invoice/receipt record or durable issuance obligation. Return the same authoritative binding and evidence IDs across retries. Conflicting replays fail. Committed acceptance is recoverable by exact replay/owner lookup. A lost response never authorizes a second order. There is no provider attempt, fake collection or separate acceptance event. PDF rendering/download is outside confirmation.

Source today: `ReservationCommercialClient` uses Payment envelopes and configurable machine credentials for preview/quote/order with a collection lookup method. `OperationsServiceAuthentication` and the internal reservation controller supply service-auth source; deployment remains unverified. `ReservationQuote` carries VehicleId and coverage version. Order replay checks persisted quote ID/version and order scope/amount/expiry, but `PersistedAcceptedRequest` echoes caller currency and method. Verify all immutable fields and replay/expiry before relying on the response. Invoice entities/mapping/read/PDF paths exist; none proves that the covered order writes receipt evidence. The Operations zero-price adapter throws `OWNER_UNAVAILABLE`.

### Covered acceptance recovery

| Scenario | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Operations commits, then dies before delivery | The transactional outbox retains the trigger; the existing bus delivery path dispatches after recovery. |
| Payment accepted but response was lost, or the consumer restarts | Keep outcome unknown. Replay exact operation or use the agreed authoritative lookup. Persist the original evidence once before guarded confirmation. |
| Duplicate trigger | Use the same operation key and binding; local transition and Payment replay are idempotent. |
| Temporary Payment unavailability | Classify retryable failures and apply the supported MassTransit retry/redelivery budget. Keep pending/unknown evidence and the stable identity. |
| Budget exhausted | Preserve observable pending/unknown or definitive outcome as supported by evidence; use existing supported fault/operations reconciliation and redrive with the same identity. Verify the durable recovery route before enablement. Short retry wiring alone gives no prolonged-outage guarantee. |
| Quote or entitlement invalid | Reject stale review before commit where detectable. A definitive post-hold rejection cannot confirm; reconcile/release with applicable claim rules and require renewed review for another booking. Preserve uncertain effects for lookup. |
| H expires before acceptance | Covered H is `min(hold creation + effective reservation hold duration, T)`. At H, absent an eligible committed decision from a guarded evaluation before H, expire/release the covered hold and require renewed review and a new admission request. No paid-style reacquisition. Recover possibly committed commercial evidence for reconciliation only. |
| Acceptance arrives after cancellation or expiry | Guard current state, suppress booking resurrection, and reconcile the linked zero-value commercial record/receipt with Payment. No payout without collected money. |

Audit the acknowledgement path explicitly in the confirmation slice. `AcceptZeroPriceTermsHandler` catches unavailable/unknown failures, persists deferred due state and can return normally. `ReservationHoldChangedConsumer` then finishes without forcing failure/redelivery; the existing due-work worker is a recovery path to verify. Acknowledgement without a durable executable follow-up can strand the reservation. Current hold/payment endpoints configure interval retries; their registration alone establishes neither delayed redelivery nor consumer outbox/inbox protection. Confirm exact middleware and supported recovery before retiring any scanner. No new scheduler, worker, receipt table or retry state machine is approved.

## Frontend — review, admission and confirmation slices

Allow responsive registered-car selection or existing UserService registration, slot selection and final quote review. VehicleId may remain local to selection/registration. Submit plate and slot with the exact reviewed quote and retained idempotency key. Restore ambiguous submissions with the same key. Display independent Account, vehicle, Facility and booking evaluation only from persisted results. Show payment readiness before enabling checkout. Show future subscription processing pending guarded confirmation. Use [SignalR and status-read contract](../../03-design/api/reservation-first-slice-api-design.md#signalr-progress-contract), including one scoped recovery read per initial connection, reconnect or explicit restoration. Stop reservation status loops in both checkout and payment-return phases; retain countdown-only timers if useful.

Source today: `ReservationCheckout.tsx` stores accepted terms and request replay identity and invokes `useServerRefresh` every five seconds while pending. `PaymentCheckout.tsx` polls transaction status on the same interval. `PaymentResultPage.tsx` has a repeated transaction-check path that needs reservation-flow coverage. Review the actual callers and preserve unrelated Payment flows; display-only timers are separate from network loops.

### Slice 2 — remove Operations-owned terms

Target the removal of the `ReservationTerms` entity and repository/interface, the `reservation_terms` mapping/table, legacy terms issue/accept logic and the duplicate accepted commercial JSON snapshot. Keep the review capability with Payment quote references. New bookings bind directly to immutable Payment QuoteId/version with only the Operations consent/decision evidence needed for admission, replay and verification. Retain effective Operations policy evidence, claims and booking restrictions. The target needs no replacement Operations quote table, receipt entity or pricing state machine.
