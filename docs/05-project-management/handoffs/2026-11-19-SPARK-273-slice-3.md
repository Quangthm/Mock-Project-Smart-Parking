**Deadline and trust.** The checked-in broker images have no configured delayed-message scheduler. The existing one-second recovery sweep uses persisted due times and sends through the EF outbox. Only the currently active Account attempt can succeed. Its five-second deadline, 10/30-second retry spacing, maximum of three attempts and original 90-second budget are preserved. A new attempt may receive the owner's original durable decision. An accepted Account branch switches the next wake to the overall deadline. The read-only plate and slot checks need no compensation. Definitive refusal, unavailable/malformed owner evidence, old envelope, duplicate branch success, retryable delivery and exhausted deadline have separate handling. A mismatched binding cannot advance a branch. Missing owner messages remain pending until a real recovery wake or delivered message detects expiry.

**Capacity.** The shared admission and confirmation repository query scopes future reservation conflicts to normalized plate and site across all slots. It checks half-open intervals through the stored claim end including the buffer, live payment holds, confirmed and allocated claims. Open physical visits and PARKING remain global exclusions. The confirmed-claim GiST exclusion now includes site. Operations still serializes by canonical plate before site. Facility acquired dependencies, session uniqueness, slot/site capacity and competing-writer guards remain in force. Direct SQL bypasses the C# advisory-lock and time-dependent live-hold checks. The static exclusion constraint alone cannot enforce every live-hold or physical-session case.

## SignalR and customer recovery

`/hubs/reservation-progress` requires the authenticated Driver role and closes on authentication expiry. `Subscribe(requestId)` verifies ownership before joining a server-selected Account/request group. The client subscribes first and reads `GET /api/reservation-requests/{id}/progress` once per successful initial or restored subscription. `ReservationProgress` carries snapshot identity, request revision, four check statuses, outcome, booking state, order ID/readiness, H and authorized amounts. A revision is the durable request revision plus the linked booking version. The same snapshot has the same event identity. Clients discard stale revisions, wrong-request events and prior-session callbacks. Historical PayOS returns resolve the request through `GET /api/me/reservations/by-order/{orderId}/request`; unknown and foreign orders return the same absent result. New reservation returns carry a hint and validate the returned order binding. Standalone Payment keeps its independent status behavior. Display countdowns and connection retries make no periodic reservation status reads. Future subscription commercial readiness uses the nullable `commercialReadiness` field.
