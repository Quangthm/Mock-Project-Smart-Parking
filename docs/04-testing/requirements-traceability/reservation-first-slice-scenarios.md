# Reservation first-slice acceptance scenarios

These scenarios describe product behavior, not implementation or execution evidence. References: [reservation rules](../../01-requirements/business-rules/04-reservations-allocation.md), [policy catalogue](../../01-requirements/business-rules/policy-catalogue.md), [authorization freshness](../../02-architecture/architecture-decisions/authorization-freshness-and-current-access.md).

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Durable admission before current-access evaluation | Return a receipt only after admission is durable; create no booking, claim, order or payment effect while authorization is pending. Restart resumes the same request. |
| Denied, late or mismatched authorization | Denial produces no effect. A response from an expired attempt or different actor/resource correlation cannot advance the request. The workflow has one durable decision; retries replay its original result without a fresh access evaluation. A current timely delivery envelope may carry that earlier decision timestamp. |
| Two different verified cars belonging to one Account | Both can reserve if their own resource/interval constraints pass; no Account-wide booking-count cap. |
| Same site versus different sites | Alternate formats of the same normalized plate at one site conflict on overlapping active effective intervals, including across different slots and live holds. Overlapping future reservations at different sites may pass if independently eligible. Incompatible ongoing physical occupancy still blocks under the operational guards until authoritative departure. |
| New reservation eligibility | Only the caller's verified CAR and Specific Slot mode can enter this creation flow; existing other-mode records remain readable. Requested slot is a preference, not observed location or an exact-slot guarantee. |
| Policy configuration and historical terms | Resolve approved platform/site revisions from persisted configuration; missing required values fail. Require explicit site late-tolerance enablement. Later policy changes do not rewrite accepted values or deadlines. |
| Allocation window | Evaluate allocation/recovery from A=T−effective allocation lead; backup is available during that window. No independent T−5-minute milestone is introduced. Protection and allocation windows remain distinct. |
| Facility-failure eligibility | At F, an unused confirmed unfulfilled booking earns the right. Confirmation or loss after F and before T prompts immediate recovery evaluation, then earns if unresolved. Recovery and arrival retain an earned right; actual parking or unused-booking closure ends it. Earning is not payout. |
| At booking start | Cancel an unused confirmed booking that cannot be fulfilled with one facility-failure refund intent and one notification. Preserve usable or already parked bookings. |
| Hold/confirmation deadline | H=min(creation+effective hold,T). Confirmation eligibility must be evaluated strictly before T under the required guards; expose the result only after durable commit (10 November amendment). Verified late paid money may use compatible reacquisition only before T; money arriving too late is reconciled/refunded without entitlement. Unknown outcomes remain unknown pending reconciliation. |
| Commercial acceptance | Preserve authoritative accepted versioned quote and coverage facts. Missing producers or stale terms cannot silently become zero-price approval or invented coverage. |
| Physical entry/departure and audit | Actual entry requires a usable allocation; requested preference alone cannot authorize entry. Payment preparation does not mean physical departure. Persist state change and audit event atomically; no event on rejected/rolled-back writes or completed departure replay. |
| Audit consumer unavailable | Configured durable capture survives broker interruption/restart; consumer deduplicates event IDs. Missing audit configuration prevents effectful Gate writes. Audit retention belongs to the audit service. |

Read-only projections distinguish pending receipt, booking lifecycle, payment/refund, arrival, actual parking, physical departure, requested resource and observed location. No status shortcut may imply an unverified effect.

## Admission, realtime and commercial acceptance

| Scenario | Expected result | Requirement / rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review and consent | Resolve site/tenant by slot; quote the exact bound intent with initial coverage false. No review establishes a hold. Changed selection, expiry or coverage/version mismatch at guarded admission requires renewed quote and consent. | FR-RES-01/04, FR-POL-06; BR-POL-04, BR-PAY-01 |
| Plate ownership before and after booking | Absent/inactive/ambiguous/other-Account or transferred-away before the owner check fails admission. Transfer/removal after accepted booking alone leaves booking and original Account access intact; another Account knowing the plate cannot read/modify it. Trusted gate evidence resolves the physical vehicle. | FR-RES-01/05; BR-RES-01 |
| Idempotent submission | Same Account/key/canonical intent returns original request and at most one reservation and hold, including after a lost 202. Changed intent on the key conflicts. Another Account cannot read the original receipt. | FR-RES-01/12; BR-RES-07 |
| Parallel fail-fast | Force definitive vehicle rejection, then deliver late Account and Facility successes, duplicate and stale-attempt replies. Receipt stays rejected with no claim/order. In a separate run, exhaust an unavailable branch and verify dependency outcome. | FR-RES-01/12; BR-RES-07 |
| SignalR recovery | Disconnect while a check and then a reservation transition commit. Rejoin and issue one scoped authoritative status read per reconnect. Interleave duplicate and out-of-order notifications; retain newer revision. Verify denied subscription across Accounts and no timed network status requests through checkout/return. | FR-RES-05/10/12; BR-RES-07 |
| Concurrent capacity competition | Race requests for incompatible capacity. Exactly one compatible claim wins where capacity admits one. Confirm same-plate/site admission serializes across slots and separately test cross-site future eligibility against an active physical session. | FR-RES-02/03, FR-CAP-03; BR-RES-02/06 |
| Crash after hold commit | Kill Operations after commit and before dispatch. On restart the outbox delivers the hold trigger; provisioning reuses one operation ID. Admission remains terminal. | FR-RES-04/12; BR-RES-07, BR-PAY-04 |
| Lost covered acceptance response (future) | Commit acceptance at Payment then drop reply and restart consumer. Show pending/unknown, replay identical key, recover stable order and receipt evidence, then guard confirmation once. | FR-RES-04/12, FR-PAY-04/05; BR-PAY-04 |
| Duplicate or changed zero request (future) | Concurrent identical calls replay one linked zero-value order, acceptance and receipt/obligation. Changed payload conflicts; no provider attempt/collection. | FR-RES-04, FR-PAY-04/05; BR-PAY-04 |
| Covered progress (future) | Committed protected hold shows PROCESSING_SUBSCRIPTION; Payment acceptance alone leaves confirmation pending. Only guarded committed Operations confirmation produces CONFIRMED. No PDF rendering dependency. | FR-RES-04/05/12; BR-RES-07 |
| Unavailable commercial owner or retry exhaustion (future) | Preserve stable identity and unknown evidence, verify fault/manual redrive and no stranded acknowledged message. Definitive stale/invalid acceptance cannot confirm. | FR-RES-04/12, FR-PAY-04; BR-PAY-04 |
| Expired/invalidated claim | Race expiry, Facility edit/Gate change and paid or covered evidence. Paid evidence may use compatible reacquisition only under the approved guards strictly before T. Future covered acceptance needs valid protection and matching evidence at the guarded evaluation strictly before H. At H, after expiry or cancellation, reconcile later evidence and require new review/submission. A qualifying pre-deadline decision is visible only after durable commit. | FR-RES-04/06/12; BR-RES-03/07, BR-PAY-04 |
| Paid payOS | Pay against the real order issued with exact H and immutable quote binding. Browser redirect/QR/client success cannot confirm. An agreed versioned collection event with distinct financial identity and immutable quote binding permits one guarded transition. Wrong order/amount/currency, duplicate and late money follow rejection/dedup/reconciliation. | FR-RES-04, FR-PAY-03/04; BR-PAY-03/04 |

See the [decision](../../05-project-management/decision-log/2026-11-18-SPARK-273-reservation-contract-amendment.md) and [deployment verification](../reports/SPARK-273-deployment-checklist.md). ## Facility-failure reason visibility

[AC-139](acceptance-scenarios.md#28-november-mvp-alignment) verifies that a Driver can see the committed status and authoritative recorded reason for their own reservation through authorized history/detail or supported notification. The implementation gap is tracked separately from production cancellation writers. Execution is **NOT RUN**.
