## Implemented scope and prior evidence

Operations owns the named CAR slot, site/plate guards, entitlement, durable admission progress and Quartz deadlines. User owns current Account and plate eligibility. Facility owns slot metadata, interval eligibility and structure facts. Payment owns immutable QuoteId evidence, deposit order, checkout, settlement, receipt and financial disposition. The frontend uses owner-scoped reads and SignalR for reservation progress. The gate and session workflows use their existing checkout.

The 202 response records admission intent. Payment collects positive deposits against the original H, and Operations confirms or reconciles the authoritative collection. H is the accepted unpaid-hold deadline capped at the reservation start T. D is the admission deadline, including final evaluation, with a 15-second default and whole-second overrides from 1 to 90. Zero-deposit admission commits confirmation without H, then links the Payment order and receipt asynchronously.

Keep deadline processing available while genuine outstanding obligations exist. [Cutover queries and queue checks](SPARK-273-deployment-checklist.md#checkpoint-d-coordinated-cutover-and-recovery)

The executable checks the job in the reservation group, its type and the absence of triggers, and queues an immediate firing. It takes one exact job name. ```

For failed registration, use the checklist's single-envelope recovery with original AMQP properties, message ID, correlation, subject and D/H. Confirm the resulting job or terminal business state before resolving the error copy. Retain existing data and queue contents, and route populated-schema changes through the deployment owner.
